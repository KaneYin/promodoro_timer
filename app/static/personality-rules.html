How we choose the label

ResultRule
Time-window personalityAfter both data minimums are met: the leading window has ≥50% of focus time and leads the runner-up by ≥15 percentage points.
🎨 Flexible FocuserEnough data, but no window meets both conditions.
🔎 Discovering Your RhythmSome focus time, but either data minimum is not met.
🌱 No Focus YetNo completed focus time in this period.

Daily periods run midnight to midnight; weeks run Monday to Sunday; months follow the calendar. Current periods show results so far.

Shares use focus duration, not session counts or daily-label votes. Completed sessions are split across time windows and midnight; pauses and breaks are excluded for new records. Older records use estimated timing, disclosed in Details.

Classification uses unrounded shares; the pie chart shows rounded percentages. Chart and ranking totals use session completion dates, so they may differ from the personality breakdown at calendar boundaries.
