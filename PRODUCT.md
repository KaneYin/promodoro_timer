# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Two trusted friends who compare focused time and computer usage. Each uses a Mac, mostly in a quiet room, often for long study or work blocks. They open the page, press Start, and want the screen to get out of the way.

## Purpose

A small personal Pomodoro and count-up focus timer with friendly Focus and Screen Time leaderboards. Success is that the timer feels calm to begin, nothing competes for attention while it runs, and the stats are there when the session ends.

## Surface modes

Operate. The timer is the tool; comparison, charts and leaderboards are secondary and appear only when the user is not mid-session.

## Brand and tone

Calm, unhurried, quietly warm. Never urgent, gamified or loud. Competition is friendly and stays out of the way during a session.

## Constraints

- No change to behaviour, element ids, data flow or copy that carries meaning (see docs/frontend-backend.md).
- Static HTML, CSS and ES modules served by FastAPI. No build step, no external network assets.
- Keyboard and screen-reader support must remain; light and dark both supported.

## Anti-references

Tomato-red urgency, streak and confetti gamification, dashboards of tiles, neon dark mode.
